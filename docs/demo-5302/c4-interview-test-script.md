> Welcome to OIA. Please describe a recent portfolio analysis you led and how you communicated risks to senior stakeholders.

## 第 1 轮

> Last quarter I led the **quarterly portfolio variance analysis** for our **sovereign wealth fund subsidiary**. I rebuilt the **sector concentration model** in Excel and flagged a **12% overweight in financials** versus our **investment policy limit**. I presented the findings to the **investment committee** with a one-page **executive narrative** and a **risk heat map** showing **liquidity** and **credit exposure** by counterparty.

## 第 2 轮

> To quantify impact, I stress-tested the **financials overweight** under a **200 basis point** rate shock and estimated **OMR 4.2 million** potential **mark-to-market** loss. I compared that to our **risk appetite threshold** of **OMR 6 million** and recommended a **partial rebalance** over six weeks rather than an immediate fire sale, because **market liquidity** in two **GCC bank** names was thin.

## 第 3 轮

> Before the committee meeting, I validated the model with **Finance** and **Risk** — they asked me to add **sensitivity tables** for **FX** and **oil price** scenarios. In the session I walked the **CFO** through the **base case vs downside case**, and we agreed to cap **single-name exposure** at **8%** until the next **board review**.
