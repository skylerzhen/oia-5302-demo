# OIA Demo — Senior Financial Analyst

**Department:** Investment Portfolio Management  
**Level:** Senior professional  
**Data classification:** Synthetic demonstration content only  
**Integration label:** Jadarah write-back is a **simulated endpoint**

## Role purpose

Support portfolio-management decisions by analysing performance, explaining
variance and risk exposure, maintaining controlled financial models, and
communicating evidence-based findings to Arabic- and English-speaking
stakeholders.

## Core responsibilities

1. Analyse portfolio performance, allocation and concentration.
2. Prepare quarterly variance and risk reports for senior stakeholders.
3. Maintain controlled Excel models with traceable assumptions.
4. Explain investment findings in Arabic and English.
5. Escalate material risk, liquidity or forecast-threshold exceptions.

## Evidence-linked evaluation rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Minimum evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Portfolio analysis | 20% | A concrete portfolio or allocation analysis example |
| Risk reporting | 15% | Risk measure, variance, threshold or escalation example |
| Financial modelling | 15% | Controlled Excel model or forecasting workflow |
| Finance experience | 15% | Five or more relevant years |
| Relevant degree | 15% | Finance, accounting, economics, MBA or MSc |
| Arabic and English communication | 20% | Evidence of bilingual reporting or stakeholder communication |

## Demo acceptance threshold

- **Qualified:** 60% or above, with at least four of six dimensions evidenced.
- **Shortlist recommendation:** HR must enter a reason and explicitly submit.
- **Disqualification:** HR must enter a reason and explicitly submit.
- **Final outcome:** AI may recommend only; HR confirms the final decision.
